## YouTube description

More than fifteen hundred golden toads gathered to breed in a Costa Rican cloud forest in nineteen eighty-seven. Two years later, only one was seen.

This documentary follows the golden toad through its tiny mountain home, its extraordinary colours, its brief breeding season, the failed pools of nineteen eighty-seven, the last confirmed sighting, and the difficult scientific search for a cause.

## Pinned comment

The golden toad's exact extinction mechanism is still debated. An exceptional dry period and chytrid disease are leading explanations, and they may have interacted. What is certain is the speed of the documented fall: more than fifteen hundred adults in nineteen eighty-seven, then one last confirmed male in nineteen eighty-nine.
