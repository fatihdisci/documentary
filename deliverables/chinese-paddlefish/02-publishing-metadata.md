## YouTube description

A giant fish once moved through China's longest river. The Chinese paddlefish had a long paddle-shaped snout, a powerful body, and a life built around one great river journey.

This documentary follows the fish through deep water, feeding grounds, long trips upstream, heavy fishing, a dam that cut off its route, the last confirmed sighting in twenty-oh-three, and the searches that found nothing.
