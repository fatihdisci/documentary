## YouTube description

Passenger pigeons once crossed North America in flocks so vast that witnesses said the sky darkened for hours. Their population was often estimated in the billions. Then, within a few decades, the wild flocks disappeared.

This documentary follows the evidence behind one of history's most dramatic extinctions. It explores the bird's nomadic life, its vast nesting colonies, the rise of commercial hunting, the loss of hardwood forests, and the final years of Martha—the last known passenger pigeon.

Martha died at Cincinnati Zoo on 1 September 1914. Her death proved that even the most abundant species can vanish when warning signs are ignored.
