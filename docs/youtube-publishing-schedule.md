# Global English YouTube Publishing Schedule

Timezone: `Europe/Istanbul` (Türkiye saati, UTC+3).

Each documentary runs on a two-day release cycle. Publish the long video first on Day 0, then release its three supporting Shorts across the next 25 hours. All three Shorts must relate to the Day 0 long-form documentary and link to it only after it is public.

| Relative time | Local time | Content |
|---|---:|---|
| Day 0 | 22:00 | Long-form documentary |
| Day 0 | 23:00 | Short 1 — strongest extinction or mystery hook; points viewers to the long video |
| Day 1 | 18:00 | Short 2 — discovery, setting, or species hook |
| Day 1 | 23:00 | Short 3 — ecological angle, legacy, or secondary hook |

The next documentary starts at 22:00 on Day 2 and follows the same pattern. This equals one long video every two days, with three related Shorts over the following 25 hours. In a calendar week this is normally three or four long videos, averaging 3.5 long videos and 10.5 Shorts per week.

## Why these times

The times are a fixed Türkiye-time compromise for Europe and North America. At 22:00 in Türkiye, it is evening in the UK and central Europe while it is afternoon in the United States. The first Short follows at 23:00, once the long video is already public. The 18:00 Day 1 Short reaches Europe in late afternoon and the US around late morning or lunch; the final 23:00 Short reaches Europe in the evening and the US in the afternoon or early evening. The exact conversion changes with daylight saving time, but the audience windows stay useful without seasonal rescheduling.

| Türkiye time | UK / Central Europe, summer | US Eastern / Pacific, summer | UK / Central Europe, winter | US Eastern / Pacific, winter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18:00 | 15:00 / 16:00 | 11:00 / 08:00 | 15:00 / 16:00 | 10:00 / 07:00 |
| 22:00 | 19:00 / 20:00 | 15:00 / 12:00 | 18:00 / 19:00 | 14:00 / 11:00 |
| 23:00 | 20:00 / 21:00 | 16:00 / 13:00 | 19:00 / 20:00 | 18:00 / 15:00 |

After the channel has enough data, replace this starting plan with the channel's own **When your viewers are on YouTube** report in YouTube Studio.

## Notifications

YouTube limits viewers to three upload/live notifications from the same channel per rolling 24 hours. Keep notifications enabled for the long video, the strongest Day 0 Short and the strongest Day 1 Short. Publish the remaining Day 1 Short without a subscriber notification.

## Upload requirements

- Viewer-facing metadata is English.
- Upload videos as private with a scheduled `publishAt` time.
- Include title, description, tags, category `Education`, default language `en`, and `selfDeclaredMadeForKids: false` unless the individual video is specifically made for children.
- Before retrying an interrupted upload, check the existing channel videos to avoid duplicates.
